# Who this is for

GBP Scorecard is for **local home-service businesses** and the people who market them.

## The trades

Plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, pest control, garage door, landscaping, restoration, cleaning, moving, locksmith, pool service, and similar local trades where:

- the customer has a local problem or project,
- the business serves a city, county, or service radius,
- the next step is usually a call, message, estimate request, or booking,
- trust and response speed matter more than a content library.

## The people

- **Owners and operators** who want to know why a visible profile is not producing enough calls or booked jobs.
- **In-house marketers** who need a repeatable diagnosis before proposing work.
- **Agencies and consultants** who need a defensible, evidence-labeled second opinion.
- **Analysts and developers** who want machine-readable findings for a larger system.

## Not a fit

- SaaS, e-commerce, media, and national brands with mature analytics teams.
- Businesses with no public profile and no intention to create one.
- Anyone looking for a guaranteed revenue lift, an automated fix, or a ranking guarantee.
- Anyone who wants a field-completion checklist with a score attached.

## What the framework assumes

1. The Google Business Profile is a **public storefront**, not the whole business.
2. A customer journey continues after Google: call, message, website, booking, estimate, response.
3. Some evidence is observable; some is not. The difference must be explicit.
4. Owner data is valuable. It is not a score penalty.
5. A profile that cannot produce a next action is more commercially exposed than a profile missing an optional field.

## The boundary

This scorecard diagnoses the **public GBP**. It does not diagnose:

- the website's mobile form or page speed ([Website Scorecard](https://thatguynef.github.io/website-scorecard/)),
- Local Pack rank or grid visibility ([Local Visibility Scorecard](https://thatguynef.github.io/local-visibility-scorecard/)),
- CRM response time, close rate, or revenue (owner data or authorized connected diagnostics),
- the operational remediation itself (RAD).
