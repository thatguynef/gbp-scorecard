## Scoring model

For each category: normalized = category.max * earned / applicable_max. Total = sum(normalized) * 100 / sum(category.max for categories with applicable checks). Category caps apply to normalized category points; overall caps apply to the total. Final = min(total, overall cap).

**Rounding.** Category normalized values are rounded to 2 decimals. Total and final are rounded to 1 decimal. Display = final rounded to the nearest integer. Bands use the display score.

**Owner data.** Owner-data questions are never scored and never reduce the public score.

**Evidence.** Every applicable check needs evidence and confidence verified or strong_signal. A deterministic check must be verified.

Can a prospective customer confidently understand who this company is, where it operates, when it is available, and whether its public information is coherent?

#### GBP-ID-01 — Business status and identity (2 points)

**Stage:** identify · **Severity:** critical · **Evaluation:** deterministic · **Applicability:** required · RAD: `gbp_optimization`

**Principle:** A closed or misidentified listing cannot serve a customer.

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 2 | The profile is operational and the business name matches the trade and destination website. |
| 1 | The profile is temporarily closed, or status/name cannot be verified from available public data. |

### F. Handoff & Journey Continuity — 10 points

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|

| 1 | The destination resolves but has a slow, insecure, or confusing redirect. |
| 0 | The destination is dead, unreachable, or redirects to unrelated content. |

**Evidence to capture:** destination fetch, final URL, certificate

#### GBP-HND-02 — Destination identity matches the profile (2 points)

**Stage:** handoff · **Severity:** high · **Evaluation:** model_judgment · **Applicability:** required · requires: `website_resolves` · RAD: `gbp_optimization`

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 2 | The destination clearly represents the same business name, brand, and location. |
| 1 | The connection is plausible but one identity signal is weak or missing. |
| 0 | The destination appears to belong to a different business or a directory. |

**Recommended intervention:** Align the business name, logo, and location across Google and the site.

#### GBP-HND-03 — Phone, hours, and service signals stay consistent (2 points)

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|

## Hard caps

A cap is exceptional. It exists only where an observable failure can invalidate the rest of the diagnosis.

| Cap | Scope | Cap | Deterministic trigger | Rationale |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| The profile is marked permanently closed (`profile_closed`) | overall | 25 | profile_status = CLOSED_PERMANENTLY | A permanently closed profile can stop nearly every customer action. This is the only status cap; temporary closure is a strong signal, not a punitive cap. |
| No meaningful customer action path (`no_action_path`) | overall | 30 | phone_action_usable = False; website_action_usable = False; booking_action_usable = False; other_action_usable = False | If a customer cannot call, visit the site, book, or use another action, the profile cannot complete its job. |
| Major business-identity mismatch (`major_identity_mismatch`) | category A | 6 | identity_consistent = False | A material name, category, or destination mismatch makes every other identity signal unreliable. Caps Identity & Accuracy at 6/15. |
| Broken primary website action (`primary_site_broken`) | category F | 4 | website_destination_state in ['dead', 'unrelated', 'insecure'] | A broken primary website handoff is a handoff failure. It caps Handoff & Journey Continuity at 4/10 and is not also applied as a Conversion cap. |
| Dead booking destination presented as a primary action (`dead_booking_primary`) | category C | 10 | booking_is_primary_action = True; booking_destination_state in ['dead', 'unrelated'] | Advertising a dead booking destination is worse than not advertising booking. Caps Conversion & Actionability at 10/22. |

Category caps apply to the normalized category points before the overall cap. A cap is applied once. The verifier recomputes every trigger from `facts`.

## Owner-data questions (never scored)

These questions require CRM, phone, booking, messaging, or financial data. They do not reduce the public score.

| ID | Stage | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| OD-CALL-ANSWER-RATE | response | Call answer rate | The profile can produce a call, but only the phone system can show whether it was answered. |
| OD-SPEED-TO-LEAD | response | Speed to lead | Response time affects whether a ready customer books; public data cannot prove it. |
| OD-BOOKING-CONVERSION | book | Booking conversion | A working booking link does not prove a booked job. |
